About

Research Profile

Qiang Zhi is an Associate Professor and Master's Supervisor at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science, Jiangsu Normal University, where he serves as Head of the Department of Computer Science and Technology. He received his M.Eng. from Hiroshima University in 2016 and his Ph.D. in Engineering from Nagoya University in 2020. He subsequently conducted research at the School of Engineering, Tokyo Institute of Technology, and the Tokyo office of the Institute of Electronics, Information and Communication Engineers (IEICE).

His research focuses on privacy-preserving and verifiable data management, network and system security, and trustworthy federated learning and collaborative intelligence, with applications in vehicular networks, edge computing, and the Industrial Internet. He has published more than 40 academic papers in journals and conferences including IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering, IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management, Information Sciences, and Computer Networks.

Research Focus

Secure and Trustworthy Networked Intelligence

01

Network & System Security

Attack-surface modeling, security decisions, resource optimization, and resilient control for vehicular networks, edge computing, and the Industrial Internet.

02

Trustworthy Federated Learning

Personalized, robust, and privacy-preserving federated learning, contribution assessment, and verifiable collaboration among distributed participants.

03

Privacy-Preserving Data Management

Encrypted search, fine-grained access control, data-sharing audits, and verifiable computing for cross-organization data and knowledge management.
